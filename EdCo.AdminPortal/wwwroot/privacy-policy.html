Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | Application: StudyPro (EdCo)

Welcome to StudyPro, operated by EdCo Educational Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering for the mobile app or using our features:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, student ID, and encrypted password.
  • Educational Data: Quiz responses, study progress, subject enrollments, and flashcard performance.
  • Uploaded Media: Images or camera captures submitted for automated AI grading and quantitative tutor assistance.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system, and diagnostic crash logs (via Sentry telemetry).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to provide and improve educational services:

  • To authenticate your identity and synchronize study progress across devices.
  • To process AI tutor inquiries and automated grading evaluations.
  • To diagnose software crashes and maintain high platform availability.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

StudyPro leverages secure Artificial Intelligence services (such as Groq AI and Gemini Vision) to provide automated grading and tutoring. Media submitted for grading is processed securely and is never sold or used for public AI training models.

4. Data Storage & Security

We enforce industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and encrypted data storage at rest. User credentials and authorization tokens are protected using standard security protocols.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To request complete account and data deletion, please contact our support team at privacy@edco.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at privacy@edco.com or contact EdCo Customer Support.
